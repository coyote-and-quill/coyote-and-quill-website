The AI Readiness Self-Audit

Eight questions. Eight dimensions. Every “no” is a gap.

Most companies buy the AI tool and skip everything around it. But the tool is one of eight dimensions of readiness — and the other seven are where AI adoption actually succeeds or fails. Answer honestly.

  1. Doctrine

    Is there a written rule for where AI can and cannot be used?

    No → every department guesses differently.

    YesNo

  2. Organization

    Can you produce a current list of every AI effort underway across the company?

    No → you’re running more experiments than you know about.

    YesNo

  3. Training

    Can you show each person’s proficiency level and what they’re cleared to do with AI?

    No → everyone has a login; no one has a training record.

    YesNo

  4. Leadership

    When an AI effort stalls, does leadership diagnose the organizational gap — or blame the technology?

    No → you’ll keep buying tools to fix what tools can’t.

    YesNo

  5. Materiel

    Have you audited which AI tools you own, who uses them, and for what?

    No → you’re paying for capability you can’t see or govern.

    YesNo

  6. Personnel

    Can you identify who’s already using AI — including the unsanctioned use?

    No → your best innovators are working in the closet.

    YesNo

  7. Facilities

    Is the approved AI path easier to use than the shadow workaround?

    No → people take the easy path, and the easy path is ungoverned.

    YesNo

  8. Policy

    For each function using AI, can you name which regulations apply?

    No → you’re deploying inside constraints you haven’t mapped.

    YesNo

Reading it

0–2 no’s — you’re ahead of most. Close the gaps and scale.   3–5 — the tool isn’t your problem; the readiness around it is.   6+ — you’re buying AI without the org to employ it.

This isn’t a score — it’s a work order. Build Policy first, Materiel last.
