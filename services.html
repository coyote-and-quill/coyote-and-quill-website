AI Adoption & Integration

Most organizations approach AI the wrong way — they buy a tool, deploy it in a sandbox, and call it transformation. Coyote & Quill does the opposite: assess the organization's actual capability state first, identify where AI creates compounding value, build the integration architecture, and train the people who have to live with it.

Strategy and execution. Operational transformation is the outcome.

What's included

  • → AI readiness assessment using the DOTMLPF-P framework adapted for commercial organizations
  • → Integration strategy — connecting AI tools to existing workflows, systems, and data
  • → Change management — leadership coaching, staff enablement, and adoption measurement
  • → Custom tooling — where no commercial solution fits, we build it
